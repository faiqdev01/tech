How much does it cost?

The price depends on the scope, so I quote each project individually. Message me your idea on WhatsApp and I will send you a clear quote.

Yes. When the project is finished you receive the complete source code, so the app is fully yours.

Can you publish the app on Google Play and the App Store?

Yes. I help with store listings, builds and the release process for both Android and iOS.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

I offer support and maintenance, so bugs get fixed and the app stays up to date with new Flutter and OS versions.

Can you work with my existing design or app?

Yes. I can build from your Figma or XD designs, or take over and improve an existing Flutter project.
